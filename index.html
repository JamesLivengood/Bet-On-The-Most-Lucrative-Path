DFS Maze Build Semi-Slow Speed

  • Goal: Get Rich
  • How: Each round, click the bottom buttons to choose your path
  • Start: click the yin-yang
  • End Game / Cash Out: click the cash out button and record your score
  • **Once you cash out you must re-start to play again
  • - After the maze builds, you have 10 seconds to choose your path
  • - If you have not chosen within the 10 seconds, you will be left out of the round, and will automatically lose 1000 dollars
  • - Each block's worth is determined by how DARK BLUE, it is, the DARKER the blue, the MORE 'blue hundreds' on the block
  • - Therefore, you want to pick the maze build who's solve will pass through the highest amount of DARK BLUE blocks
  • About: The maze builder uses a Depth First Search to create the maze. It searches for a random, neighboring, unvisited node, sets the parent-child relationship between the current node and the next node, and then builds the needed walls to create the maze. To solve the path, it accesses the nodes, and uses a Breadth First Search, to find the shortest path to the goal.
